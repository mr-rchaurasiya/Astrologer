2. `CAREER_REPORT`: Dashamsha (D10) professional potential, authority yogas, dasha timing.
3. `MARRIAGE_REPORT`: Navamsha (D9) relationship alignment, 7th house lord, Kuja Dosha.
4. `EDUCATION_REPORT`: Chaturvimsamsa (D24) academic and exam horizon.
5. `FINANCE_REPORT`: Hora (D2) and 2nd/11th house wealth indicators.
6. `YEARLY_FORECAST`: 12-month Gochar transits, Sade Sati phases, Vimshottari progression.
7. `DASHA_REPORT`: In-depth analysis of running Mahadasha and Antardasha.
8. `TRANSIT_REPORT`: Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu/Ketu transit impacts.
9. `COMPATIBILITY_REPORT`: 36-Point Ashtakoota Milan and Mangal Dosha Samyam.
