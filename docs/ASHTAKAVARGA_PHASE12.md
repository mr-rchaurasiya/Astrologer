# Ashtakavarga Engine: BAV & SAV Matrix (Phase 12)

## 1. Overview
The Ashtakavarga system (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra Ch. 66–72) quantifies the planetary benefic contributions across the 12 signs of the zodiac based on the natal positions of the 7 major Grahas and Lagna.

## 3. Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)
- **Grand Total**: $48 + 49 + 39 + 54 + 56 + 52 + 39 = \mathbf{337 \text{ Bindus}}$.
- **Average Per Sign**: $337 / 12 \approx 28 \text{ Bindus}$.
- **Sign Interpretation**:
  - $\ge 30 \text{ Bindus}$: Highly auspicious, strong vitality, excellent transit fruits.
  - $28 - 29 \text{ Bindus}$: Balanced, neutral.
  - $< 25 \text{ Bindus}$: Vulnerable, demanding caution during transits through that sign.
