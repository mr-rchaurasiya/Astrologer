# Shadbala (Six-Fold Planetary Strength) Engine (Phase 12)

## 1. Overview
Shadbala (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra Ch. 27) measures the quantitative potency of the seven physical planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) using six distinct astronomical and astrological criteria.

---

## 2. The Six Sources of Strength

| Bala Component | Sanskrit Name | Domain Evaluated | Max Scale (Virupas) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Sthana Bala** | Positional Strength | Uchcha Bala (Exaltation), Kendradi, Saptavargiya dignity | ~ 150 - 250 |
| **2. Dig Bala** | Directional Strength | Cardinal house alignment (Jup/Mer 1st, Sun/Mars 10th, Sat 7th, Moon/Ven 4th) | 60 |
| **3. Kala Bala** | Temporal Strength | Day/Night birth (Nathonnatha), Paksha, Hora lordship | ~ 80 - 150 |
| **4. Cheshta Bala** | Motional Strength | Retrograde status, planetary velocity vs mean speed | 60 |
| **5. Naisargika Bala** | Natural Strength | Fixed astronomical brilliance (Sun: 60 down to Sat: 8.57) | 8.57 - 60 |
| **6. Drik Bala** | Aspectual Strength | Net benefic vs malefic aspectual virupas | ~ 10 - 45 |
