# AI Conversation Intelligence, Summarization & Feedback Architecture

## 1. Overview
Transforms raw conversational exchanges into actionable structured insights and gives users fine-grained control over AI responses.

## 2. Structured Summarization
- Triggered periodically or on-demand for consultation sessions.
- Extracts:
  - Key Astrological Concerns (e.g., career transition, marriage compatibility, health vitality).
  - Prescribed Vedic Remedies (e.g., gemstones, mantras, charity, fasting).
  - Planetary Influences (dominant dashas, transit Gochar factors).

## 3. Saved Consultations Vault
- Allows users to bookmark key consultations with custom tags (`#career`, `#saturn`, `#remedy`), personal notes, and favorite filters.
- Supports search, filtering, and archiving.

## 4. Personalization Settings
- Users can customize:
  - **Astrology Terminology**: Standard Vedic, Traditional Sanskrit, or Simplified.
  - **Response Style**: Concise, Balanced, or In-Depth / Philosophical.
  - **Language**: English, Hindi, or Sanskrit.

## 5. Feedback Loop & Telemetry
- Users can rate individual AI messages (`helpful` / `not_helpful`) with categorical feedback (`accuracy`, `clarity`, `depth`, `hallucination`, `tone`).
- Aggregated in the admin dashboard for continuous AI prompt calibration.
